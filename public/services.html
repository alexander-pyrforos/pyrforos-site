Data services for teams that sell

Two services, built and run by one engineer. A weekly lead feed that catches local businesses right after a fresh 1–2 star public review lands — the moment they need reputation help. And AI data structuring: LLM pipelines that turn messy unstructured text into clean, database-ready records.

Lead feed

A weekly CSV for sales and reputation-management teams.

  • Business name, category, current public rating and total review count
  • The fresh 1–2 star review: date, text excerpt, direct link to that exact review
  • Phone, website, address
  • Owner/manager name where public (Business and Professional)
  • Contact email where publicly available

AI data structuring

LLM pipelines for unstructured text — built, fine-tuned, self-hosted.

Fine-tuned open-weight models turn messages, documents and catalogs into clean structured records — classification, extraction and enrichment at pipeline scale, running on your own infrastructure.

  • Fields decided with you — exactly what your downstream systems need
  • Output as CSV, JSON or database-ready inserts
  • Fine-tuned open-weight models or frontier APIs, as the job fits
  • Runs on-prem or in an EU cloud

Project-based pricing — scoped per pipeline, no fixed tiers.
